# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Lifelong self-directed learners, software engineers, career switchers, students, and technical professionals aiming to master complex skills through structured, time-bound, and adaptive blueprints.

## Product Purpose
Transform natural language goals into adaptive, sequential, month-by-month learning roadmaps powered by Google Gemini, featuring milestone tracking, curated resources, conceptual verification quizzes, guardrailed AI mentoring, and adaptive rescheduling on a strict $0 free-tier architecture.

## Positioning
Unlike static course checklists or generic AI chat threads, Milestone AI turns any goal into an active, verifiable curriculum with pacing guardrails, dual-model AI routing (Gemini 2.5 Flash / Pro), and 1-click schedule rebalancing that preserves completed progress when life happens.

## Operating Context
Web and desktop browsers (plus PWA mobile/tablet), typically during active study sessions, weekly planning, and task execution. Operates with local offline caching and optional Supabase backend sync.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Natural language goal processing with AI duration estimator (1–24 months).
- Overload guardrails preventing unsustainable pacing on long-term plans (>6 months blocks daily cadence).
- Structured multi-month milestone breakdowns with concrete deliverables and curated resources.
- Interactive deliverables checklists with real-time progress recalculation.
- 3-Question Micro-Quiz gate required to verify mastery before milestone completion.
- Context-aware streaming AI mentor chat with persona switching and active milestone context injection.
- 1-click adaptive rebalance schedule.
- Multi-format data export (Markdown, iCalendar, JSON).
- Strict $0 free tier requirement utilizing Supabase and Google Gemini free tiers.

## Brand Commitments
- Name: Milestone AI
- Tagline: Adaptive Learning Blueprints
- Tone: Technical, empathetic, structured, empowering, and pragmatic.
- Design Language: Modern technical dark-first interface, high readability, refined typography, and purposeful micro-interactions.

## Evidence on Hand
- Full React 19 + Tailwind CSS + Supabase implementation.
- Curated starter blueprints (Jetpack Compose, 10K Running, Conversational French, Indie Startup MVP, Machine Learning).
- Comprehensive YC-style pitch deck and technical documentation at `/docs`.

## Product Principles
1. Pacing Over Pressure: Protect learners from burnout through realistic duration estimates and overload guardrails.
2. Verified Competence: Milestones are earned through demonstrable understanding (micro-quizzes) rather than passive checkbox clicking.
3. Adaptive Continuity: Life happens; falling behind should trigger seamless schedule rebalancing, not guilt or abandonment.
4. Transparent Intelligence: Clear AI reasoning, deterministic outputs, and zero hidden lock-in.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Full WCAG AA compliance with minimum 4.5:1 text contrast, visible focus rings, aria labels on interactive controls, keyboard-traversable flows, and responsive layouts across all viewports.
